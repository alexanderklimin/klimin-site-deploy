How I see growth

Marketing can accelerate a business.

But if the structure isn't ready,
it accelerates the problems.

Real growth doesn't depend on marketing alone.

It depends on the whole structure of the business:

  • product
  • unit economics
  • acquisition channels
  • operations
  • measurement

When these pieces work together, growth becomes natural.

When they don't, the business grows… but becomes more fragile.
