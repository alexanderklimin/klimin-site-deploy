Aleksandr Klimin: from engineer to e-commerce entrepreneur in Chile

I'm Aleksandr Klimin, an e-commerce entrepreneur in Chile since 2014. I built and run two brands of my own, Ambient Lounge Chile and Eurofunda, with more than US$8M in sales combined and more than US$1M invested in Meta Ads and Google Ads. I'm also a partner in mobile apps that grew with Google Ads to more than US$100k in monthly recurring revenue. Through Klimin Marketing SpA, in Viña del Mar, I take part in growth decisions of e-commerce businesses that already sell.

A telecommunications engineer by training, but I never worked as an engineer.

My real experience was always in sales and in understanding how a business moves.

I started at 18 selling mobile phones wholesale, and then spent more than 10 years in multinational corporations as a key account executive at companies like Xerox, France Telecom and others. During my time at Xerox I discovered analytics software for audience segmentation.

That's when I started to understand how to scale sales in a more structured way.

The turning point: 2014

In 2014 I decided to change my life completely. I quit my stable corporate job and came to Chile to build an online business from scratch.

E-commerce in Chile was just getting started. I had to learn everything: I took courses, read books, made costly mistakes. Every year the ad platforms change, and I kept learning and adapting.

Building against all odds

All my products were 5 to 10 times more expensive than the market average. Nobody believed they would succeed, especially at the start.

But thanks to the right marketing moves, every product was accepted by the market. Since our brands were international, we shared experience between countries. It turned out my strategies were the most efficient, and they were applied successfully in other markets.

Cumulative results running these businesses

AMBIENT LOUNGE (first brand): I reached $500K a year in year 4 working on marketing alone. Then we scaled to $5M cumulative over 10 years.

EUROFUNDA (second brand): I went from 0 to $1M a year in just 2 years with only one person on my team. Total: $3M cumulative over 5 years.

What I learned building these businesses

After more than ten years building e-commerce businesses I understood something important.

Growth rarely depends on a single decision.

It depends on the whole structure of the business:

  • product
  • acquisition channels
  • operations
  • economics
  • measurement

When these pieces work together, growth becomes natural.

When they don't, marketing only amplifies the problems.
