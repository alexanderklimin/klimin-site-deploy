$8M in sales running my own e-commerce businesses.

Between 2015 and 2025 I built two e-commerce businesses of my own in Chile, Ambient Lounge and Eurofunda, with more than US$8M in sales combined (about CLP 6.5 billion). Ambient Lounge grew from US$50K to US$1M a year; Eurofunda went from 0 to US$1M a year in two years. The main channels were Meta Ads (average ROAS 600%), Google Ads (750%) and email marketing with Klaviyo (about 30% of sales).

It's not a linear story. It's the result of decisions, mistakes and adjustments over time.
